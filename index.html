Howdy!

I’m Jackie - an email and CRM developer with a front-end background. I build campaigns, automations, and web experiences, and I got here by a less typical route: after years as a salon director in San Francisco, I completed General Assembly’s full stack web development program in 2016 and moved into email development.

I started at Loxa Beauty, coding campaigns and triggered automations in Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) with HTML, CSS, AMPscript, SQL, XML, CSVs, and Magento.

After parting ways with Loxa, I spent eight years at Meison as the technical hand of a marketing team, supporting six DTC brands plus B2B email. I built up to 40–50 emails a week for audiences from 200 to 120K, planned and built BFCM sends at up to four emails a day per brand, and built many email automations including (but not limited to) welcome, post-purchase, winback, and sunset flows, plus tagging flows and webhook-driven automations that handled list hygiene. I also handled segmentation, personalization, A/B tests, and signup forms and landing pages in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I care about the unglamorous parts too: deliverability monitoring, CAN-SPAM compliance, ESP warming, and cross-client testing.

Klaviyo is where I know the most, from email development and segmentation to flows and integrations with Google Ads and Meta. I’ve also worked in Shopify Email, Mailchimp, Dynamic Yield, Nosto, and Justuno, so I move comfortably between email and onsite experiences and adapt quickly to new platforms.
